Bahut log sochte hain PR matlab sirf news article. Aaj ke time mein PR agency ka kaam usse kaafi bada hai. Simple mein samjho **PR agency kya karti hai** — aur **Instagram pe brand viral kaise kare**.

## PR agency kya karti hai?

1. **Story dhoondhti hai** — aapke brand ya naam mein kya newsworthy hai.
2. **Media coverage** — news portals, entertainment sites aur [paps pages](/services/pr-media-coverage) pe aapki story.
3. **Social buzz** — meme pages, influencers aur X trends se log aapke baare mein baat karein.
4. **Image banati hai** — aap kis cheez ke liye jaane jaoge, wo planned tareeke se.
5. **Reputation bachati hai** — controversy ya negative news aaye to fast response. Dekho [crisis PR](/services/crisis-pr-reputation-management).
6. **Report deti hai** — har link, screenshot aur reach ek jagah.

Celebrities, reality-show contestants, movies aur brands — sabke liye PR alag hota hai. Celebrities ke liye dekho [celebrity PR](/services/celebrity-pr).

## Instagram pe brand viral kaise kare?

- **Relatable content banao** — ad nahi, wo content jo log dost ko bhejna chahein.
- **Meme pages use karo** — ek hi din mein lakhon logon tak pahunchne ka sabse tez tareeka. Padho: [meme marketing kya hai](/blog/meme-marketing-kya-hai).
- **Trends pe fast react karo** — moment marketing ka time ghanton mein hota hai, dino mein nahi.
- **Creators ke saath collab** — sahi audience wale influencers.
- **Ek saath sab jagah dikho** — meme pages, reels, X aur paps pages same week mein; tab brand “everywhere” lagta hai.

Viral hone ka promise koi nahi de sakta, lekin sahi plan se chances kaafi badh jaate hain.


## PR agency kis kis ke liye kaam karti hai?

PR sirf bade brands ke liye nahi hai. Alag log alag kaam ke liye PR team hire karte hain:

- **Brands aur startups** — launch, trust aur media coverage ke liye.
- **Actors aur celebrities** — image, paparazzi coverage aur fan clubs ke liye.
- **Reality-show contestants** — ghar ke andar wo bol nahi sakte, bahar team unki awaaz banti hai.
- **Movies, web series aur songs** — release week ka buzz banane ke liye.
- **Founders aur creators** — personal branding aur authority ke liye.

Har case mein kaam alag hota hai, isliye achhi agency pehle aapka goal samajhti hai, phir plan banati hai.

## PR aur advertising mein kya farak hai?

Ad mein aap space khareedte ho aur khud apne baare mein bolte ho. PR mein doosre log — media, creators, meme pages — aapke baare mein baat karte hain. Isliye PR se trust zyada banta hai, aur ads se control zyada milta hai. Zyada tar brands dono ka use karte hain. Detail mein padho [PR vs advertising](/blog/pr-vs-advertising).

## PR agency ka kharcha kitna hota hai?

Koi fixed rate card nahi hota. Cost depend karti hai:

1. Ek campaign chahiye ya har mahine ka retainer
2. Kaun se platforms — news portals, paps pages, meme pages, X
3. Kitna content banana hai
4. Kitni jaldi kaam chahiye

Poori detail ke liye padho [PR agency charges in India](/blog/pr-agency-charges-in-india).

## Sahi PR agency kaise pehchaane?

Unka past kaam dekho, written plan maango, aur jo bhi “pakka viral” ka promise kare usse door raho. Poori checklist yahan hai: [PR agency kaise choose kare](/blog/pr-agency-kaise-choose-kare).


## PR ka result kab dikhta hai?

Kuch cheezein jaldi dikhti hain — paps posts aur meme page posts kuch hi dino mein live ho jaate hain. Image aur trust banne mein time lagta hai. Isliye ek campaign ke baad bhi regular PR karte raho, taaki jab koi aapko Google kare to hamesha naya aur achha content mile.
