Bigg Boss Telugu voting works in a weekly cycle: contestants are nominated in the house, a voting window opens after the nomination episode, viewers vote for the nominated contestant they want to save, and the contestant with the fewest votes is usually evicted at the weekend. In recent seasons, voting has run on the official JioHotstar app and through missed-call numbers announced by the channel.

The exact rules, like how many votes each account gets and when lines open and close, are set by the show and can change every season. So always check the official announcement. This guide explains the process, and what a [Bigg Boss PR](/services/bigg-boss-pr) team does to help a contestant’s supporters vote.

## The Bigg Boss Telugu voting process, step by step

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Nominations | Contestants nominate each other inside the house; the format changes with tasks and twists |
| 2. Voting opens | After the nomination episode airs, the voting window opens |
| 3. Fans vote | On the official app and, in recent seasons, by missed call to the contestant’s number |
| 4. Voting closes | Before the eviction episode, usually before the weekend |
| 5. Eviction | The contestant with the fewest votes is usually evicted, unless there’s a twist |

### How app voting usually works

In recent seasons, viewers opened the JioHotstar app, found the Bigg Boss Telugu page, and tapped the nominated contestant they wanted to save. Limits per account are set by the show for each season.

### How missed-call voting usually works

Each nominated contestant gets a phone number. A missed call from your phone to that number counts as a vote. Numbers change every week and every season, so only use the numbers announced by the official channel for that week.

## Finale week voting

In finale week, the voting is for the finalists, and the winner is announced on finale night. In Season 9 (2025), finale voting opened on the Sunday night after the finalists were set and closed on the Friday before the finale. Timings can change, so check the current season’s official announcement.

## Common voting myths

- **“Live voting results” online are official.** They aren’t; the show doesn’t publish counts.
- **More posts means more votes.** Posts help remind people, but only real votes count.
- **The most popular contestant online is always safe.** Online buzz and actual votes aren’t the same thing.
- **Voting rules never change.** They can change every season.

## Voting appeals in Telugu: what works

A voting appeal should be short, clear and in the audience’s language. Tell people three things: their contestant is nominated, how to vote, and when lines close. Use the contestant’s best moments from the week as clips or memes, so the appeal feels like content rather than a poster. Pin a simple Telugu “how to vote” post on every fan page so new supporters can find it.

## Wildcards and twists

Bigg Boss Telugu is known for twists: wildcard entries, no-eviction weeks, double evictions and decisions made by housemates or the host. A wildcard contestant often starts with fewer supporters, so their fan pages need to move fast from day one. When there’s a twist, keep the message calm and positive; angry posts rarely help a contestant.

## Quick glossary

- **Nominations:** contestants put in danger of eviction for the week.
- **Voting window:** the days when viewers can vote, after the nomination episode.
- **Eviction:** the contestant who leaves, usually at the weekend.
- **Wildcard:** a new contestant who enters mid-season.
- **Finale week:** the last week, when viewers vote for the winner among the finalists.
