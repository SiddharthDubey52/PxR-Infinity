Aaj kal songs pehle reels pe hit hote hain, phir streaming pe. Agar creators aapka audio use nahi kar rahe, to algorithm bhi push nahi karta. To sawaal yeh hai — **song viral kaise kare**? Is guide mein woh steps hain jo hum artists aur labels ke saath use karte hain.

## Song reels pe kyun chalte hain

Jab koi creator aapka audio use karta hai, uski reel ke neeche aapke song ka naam dikhta hai. Log click karte hain, aur dekhte hain ki aur kaun kaun use kar raha hai. Jitne zyada log use karte hain, utna zyada audio “trending” lagta hai — aur utne zyada log use karte hain. Yeh chain shuru karna hi song promotion ka kaam hai.

## Step 1: Sahi hook chuno

Poora song reels pe nahi chalta — sirf 10–15 second ka ek hissa. Woh hissa:

- **Turant pehchaan mein aaye** — catchy line ya beat.
- **Kisi action ke saath fit ho** — dance step, lip-sync, transition, joke.
- **Bina context ke bhi samajh aaye.**

Release se pehle 2–3 options try karo aur dekho kaun sa sabse natural lagta hai.

## Step 2: Reel ke liye idea do

Creators ko sirf audio mat do — ek idea do. Ek simple dance step, ek transition, ek “POV” format ya ek meme template. Jitna aasaan idea, utne zyada log try karenge. Reels ke basics ke liye padho [reel viral kaise kare](/blog/reel-viral-kaise-kare).

## Step 3: Creators ki sahi mix

- **Dance creators** — step ko popular karte hain.
- **Lip-sync aur acting creators** — lyrics ko scenes mein badalte hain.
- **Comedy creators** — audio ko meme bana dete hain.
- **Lifestyle aur travel creators** — background audio ke roop mein.

Bade creators reach dete hain, chhote creators volume dete hain. Dono chahiye. Rates samajhne ke liye padho [influencer marketing rates in India](/blog/influencer-marketing-rates-india).

## Step 4: Waves mein seeding

Sab creators ek hi din post na karein. Release day pe pehli wave, phir agle kuch dino mein aur waves — taaki audio lagatar feed mein dikhta rahe aur naye log join karte rahein.

## Step 5: Meme pages aur edits

Meme pages pe aapke song ke saath edits aur memes post hone se song un logon tak pahunchta hai jo creators ko follow nahi karte. Yeh recall ke liye bahut powerful hai.

## Step 6: Release-day buzz

- **X pe release-day trend** — fans aur fan clubs ke saath.
- **Artist ki stories aur lives** — fans ko pehle din hi stream karne ko bolo.
- **Media coverage** — music aur entertainment portals pe interview ya feature.

## Punjabi aur regional songs

**Punjabi song promotion** ya kisi bhi regional song ke liye, creators aur pages usi language aur region ke chuno. Regional audience apne creators pe zyada trust karti hai, aur wahan se song baaki India tak bhi pahunchta hai.

## Galtiyan jo song ko rokti hain

- Hook decide kiye bina promotion shuru karna.
- Sirf ek do bade creators pe poora budget.
- Release ke baad promotion turant band kar dena.
- Fake streams ya fake views — platforms pakad lete hain aur nuksaan hota hai.

## Kya har song viral hoga?

Nahi — aur koi bhi honest team yeh promise nahi karegi. Lekin sahi hook, sahi creators aur sahi timing se aapke song ko sabse achha mauka milta hai. Aur jo song viral na bhi ho, woh nayi audience zaroor banata hai.

## Results kaise measure kare

Sirf streams mat dekho. Yeh bhi track karo:

- **Reels jo aapka audio use kar rahi hain** — kitni aur kis type ke creators ki
- **Saves aur shares** jin reels pe audio laga hai
- **Search** — log aapka song ya naam search kar rahe hain ya nahi
- **Naye followers** artist ke profile pe
