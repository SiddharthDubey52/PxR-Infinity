Aaj kal har brand, creator aur actor ko PR chahiye — lekin market mein itni agencies hain ki sahi wali chunna mushkil ho jaata hai. Kuch bade promises karti hain, kuch sasti lagti hain, kuch bahut professional dikhti hain. To asli sawaal yeh hai: **PR agency kaise choose kare** jo sach mein aapke kaam aaye? Yeh guide step by step samjhati hai.

## Step 1: Pehle apna goal clear karo

Agency dhoondhne se pehle khud se poocho — PR se kya chahiye?

- **Launch ya release** — ek moment ko bada banana.
- **Image building** — log aapko kis cheez ke liye jaanein.
- **Trust** — jab koi Google kare to strong results dikhein.
- **Fan base** — reality show ya film ke liye supporters organise karna.
- **Crisis** — controversy ko handle karna.

Goal clear hoga to aap har agency se same sawaal pooch paoge aur unke jawab compare kar paoge. Agar PR basics samajhne hain to pehle padho [PR agency kya karti hai](/blog/pr-agency-kya-karti-hai).

## Step 2: Unka past kaam dekho

Website pe likha “100+ clients” kaafi nahi hai. Poocho:

1. **Case studies dikhao** — problem kya thi, kya kiya, result kya aaya.
2. **Mere jaise client ke saath kaam kiya hai?** Brand PR, celebrity PR aur reality-show PR — teeno alag skills hain.
3. **Live links aur screenshots** — asli kaam ka proof.

Humare kaam ke examples [yahan dekho](/work) — har case study mein challenge, strategy aur result likha hai.

## Step 3: Kaun kaam karega, yeh jaano

Bahut baar pitch senior log karte hain aur kaam koi aur. Poocho:

- Roz aapke account pe kaun kaam karega?
- Aapka point of contact kaun hoga?
- Kitni jaldi reply milega — khaas karke urgent time pe?

Reality show ya crisis jaise kaam mein speed sabse important hai. Agar agency 24 ghante mein reply nahi kar sakti, to wo sahi fit nahi hai.

## Step 4: Plan likhit mein lo

Achhi agency verbal promises nahi, written plan deti hai. Plan mein hona chahiye:

- Kaun se platforms — news portals, entertainment sites, paps pages, meme pages, X
- Kitni placements ya posts
- Timeline — kya kab live hoga
- Approvals ka process
- Reporting — kya milega aur kab

Isse aap alag agencies ke quotes ko seedha compare kar sakte ho. Cost samajhne ke liye padho [PR agency charges in India](/blog/pr-agency-charges-in-india).

## Step 5: Red flags pehchano

Yeh cheezein dikhein to ruk jao:

- **“Pakka viral” ya “pakka top publication” jaise promises** — koi honest agency yeh promise nahi karti.
- **Numbers jinke source nahi bataye** — “crores ki reach” bina proof ke.
- **Fake accounts ya fake engagement ki baat** — isse reputation ka nuksaan hota hai.
- **Poora paisa advance, lambe contract ke liye** — bina koi kaam dekhe.
- **Report dene se bachna** — agar woh dikha nahi sakte ki kya kiya, to shayad kiya hi nahi.

## Step 6: Chhote se start karo

Pehli baar kisi agency ke saath kaam kar rahe ho to ek focused campaign se start karo — ek launch, ek release, ya ek mahine ka plan. Kaam, communication aur report dekho. Phir lamba retainer socho.

## Traditional PR ya digital PR?

Aaj ke time mein sirf newspaper coverage kaafi nahi hai. Aapki story Instagram, X, meme pages aur creators tak bhi pahunchni chahiye. Isliye aisi agency chuno jo media coverage ke saath digital channels bhi samjhe. Detail mein padho [what is digital PR](/blog/what-is-digital-pr).

| Sawaal | Achha jawab |
| --- | --- |

| Kaun kaam karega? | Naam ke saath clear team |
| Plan? | Written, platforms aur timeline ke saath |
| Promises? | Realistic, koi “pakka result” nahi |
| Report? | Har link aur number ke saath |

## Pehli meeting mein yeh sawaal poocho

1. Mere jaise kisi client ke liye aapne kya kiya tha, aur result kya aaya?
2. Pehle 30 din mein kya live hoga?
3. Aap kaise decide karte ho ki kaun se pages ya portals mere liye sahi hain?
4. Agar plan ke hisaab se kaam na chale, to aap kya badlenge?
5. Report mein kya milega, aur kitni baar?

Jo agency in sawaalon ka seedha jawab de, wo usually kaam bhi seedha karti hai. Jo gol-mol jawab de, ya sirf bade naam ginaaye, usse aur sawaal poocho.
